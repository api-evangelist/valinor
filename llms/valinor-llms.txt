# Valinor Enterprises

> Valinor is a defense and government technology operational holding company — a "company of companies" that builds and buys mission-critical technologies to fill the gaps others ignore. It centralizes go-to-market, compliance, security, and operations while decentralizing engineering across lean, mission-driven Product Companies. Backed by General Catalyst; raised a $54M Series A led by Friends & Family Capital ($85M total raised).

Valinor publishes no public API, developer portal, or documentation surface. Its developer footprint is limited to a GitHub organization and an npm scope of packages supporting its Streamline product.

## Product Companies

- [Condor](https://www.valinor.co/products): Modular, attritable aerial system engineered for air- and ground-launch, high-volume production, and contested environments
- [Dispatch](https://www.valinor.co/products): Modular contact-charging nodes for unmanned systems, powering persistent autonomous operations; selected by DIU for the Containerized Autonomous Drone Delivery System (CADDS) program
- [Harbor](https://www.valinor.co/products): Mobile, modular medical systems for precision care, rapid response, and remote monitoring; selected by the Marine Corps for the Haven Shelter System (HSS) program
- [Reflex](https://www.valinor.co/products): Next-generation smart optics with onboard vision models, embedding intelligence at the point of capture
- [Streamline](https://www.valinor.co/products): AI-enabled data collection platform with structured ontology write-back for any user on any device, on- or offline
